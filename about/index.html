Canary in a Coal Mine scores the condition most in motion right now — your career in the AI era — against what AI is actually deploying today, not a snapshot frozen in hype. Alongside it: Cher Ami, a crisis-resource page that is free forever.

What it is

A free career risk scorer and a crisis-resource page. You answer nine questions about the work you actually do; you get a position on a map — not a doom number — and a plain-language read on where you stand. No account. No paywall. No ideology — just awareness.

The thinking behind it

Anyone can scrape a job-risk calculator. What's hard to copy is judgment about what AI can actually do today — the difference between what a vendor demo promises and what's shipping in production. A score built on hype is worse than no score. So the discipline behind the questions is simple and strict:

Every figure is dated. Every claim is sourced. When a fact can't be re-verified, it's softened rather than asserted — and when it goes stale, it comes out rather than getting a cosmetic date bump.

The editorial point of view on Career is that the honest signal is deployment reality, not capability hype — what AI is actually doing in production today, where premature rollouts are backfiring, and what that means for the person holding the job.

What it took to build

Canary is a solo project — designed, written, built, and maintained by one person. The work spans more than code:

  • Product & information design — eight pages designed and built, five live after a deliberate scope cut; a consistent scoring model and a visual system from scratch.
  • Editorial judgment — reading fast-moving technical and geopolitical news with a calm, plain-language eye, a discipline now baked into how the scorer weighs deployment reality.
  • Data integrity under change — when a live geopolitical event made the site's intelligence stale, the fix was a sourced, dated, cross-page rewrite — not a cosmetic date bump.
  • Full deployment — custom domain, hosting, clean-URL structure, email capture, and payment scaffolding, owned end to end.
