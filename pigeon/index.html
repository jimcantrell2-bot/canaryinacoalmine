About these resources and their review dates

Each entry links to provider information checked on the date shown. We reviewed the linked information and the contact details we list. We did not place test calls, submit applications, or confirm current openings. A review date is not a guarantee of availability.

Confirm hours, eligibility and any fees with the provider before travelling or committing to a service. Linked sites have their own privacy policies. Links open in this tab. Cher Ami does not provide crisis counseling or monitor requests for help.

Outside the U.S.? Find a Helpline lets you look for crisis and emotional support by country.
